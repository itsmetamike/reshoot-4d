Try the 4D camera editor

This is the Virtual Camera node's own editor, running in your browser on Example 1's real 4D capture. The camera move it opens with is the one rendered below. Change it, scrub the timeline, fly the camera, try a preset. Nothing is rendered here: rendering a move takes the full workflow and a GPU.

Best on a desktop browser with a keyboard. The capture is about 9 MB.

  • Spaceplay and pause
  • Ccamera view or editor view
  • J K Lshuttle back, stop, forward
  • W A S Dfly the camera
  • Iadd a key at the playhead
  • Escclose the editor
